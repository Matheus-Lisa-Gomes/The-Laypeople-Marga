# The Laypeople Mārga
*(Upāsaka-Dharma & The Householder's Path)*

> A structured web platform and study repository of authentic Buddhist teachings, discourses (*suttas/sūtras*), ethical frameworks, and contemplative practices tailored specifically for householders and lay practitioners living active, engaged lives in the contemporary world.

---

## ☸ Overview & Purpose

In classical Buddhist tradition, the path to liberation and peace is not confined to the monastic cloister. The Buddha gave extensive, nuanced, and pragmatic guidance to householders (*gahapatis*), tradespeople, parents, leaders, and community members. 

**The Laypeople Mārga** is dedicated to curating, organizing, and clarifying these teachings into an accessible, actionable roadmap for balancing daily responsibilities, family life, and professional vocation with deep spiritual development.
