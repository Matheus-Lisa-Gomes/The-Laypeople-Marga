# The Laypeople Mārga
*(Upāsaka-Dharma & The Householder's Path)*

> A structured web platform and study repository of authentic Buddhist teachings, discourses (*suttas/sūtras*), ethical frameworks, and contemplative practices tailored specifically for householders and lay practitioners living active, engaged lives in the contemporary world.

---

## ☸ Overview & Purpose

In classical Buddhist tradition, the path to liberation and peace is not confined to the monastic cloister. The Buddha gave extensive, nuanced, and pragmatic guidance to householders (*gahapatis*), tradespeople, parents, leaders, and community members. 

**The Laypeople Mārga** is dedicated to curating, organizing, and clarifying these teachings into an accessible, actionable roadmap for balancing daily responsibilities, family life, and professional vocation with deep spiritual development.

---

## 📚 Core Canonical Foundations

### 1. The Householder's Ethics & Relationships
* **Sigālovāda Sutta (DN 31)** — *The Layperson's Code of Conduct*: The six reciprocal relationships (parents/children, teachers/students, spouse/partner, friends/associates, workers/employers, spiritual guides/lay followers).
* **Vyagghapajja Sutta (AN 8.54)** — *Conditions of True Prosperity*: Four virtues leading to worldly well-being (initiative, stewardship, good friendship, balanced living) and four virtues leading to spiritual fulfillment (faith, virtue, generosity, wisdom).
* **Anana Sutta (AN 4.62)** — *The Four Blisses of a Householder*:
  1. *Atthi-sukha*: The bliss of rightful acquisition and honest livelihood.
  2. *Bhoga-sukha*: The bliss of righteous wealth shared with family and society.
  3. *Anaṇa-sukha*: The supreme bliss of freedom from debt.
  4. *Anavajja-sukha*: The peerless bliss of blameless, ethical conduct.

### 2. Daily Practice & Mindfulness in Action
* **The Five Precepts (*Pañcasīla*)**: The foundational ethical shield for harmonious household life.
* **Right Livelihood (*Sammā-ājīva*)**: Navigating modern work, commerce, and technology with integrity.
* **The Threefold Base of Meritorious Action (*Puññakiriyavatthu*)**:
  * *Dāna* (Cultivating open-handed generosity).
  * *Sīla* (Cultivating uprightness and harmlessness).
  * *Bhāvanā* (Cultivating mental clarity, calm, and insight amidst daily activity).
